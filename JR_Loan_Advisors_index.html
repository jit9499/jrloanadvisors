Before You Call

Straight answers.

What types of funding can JR Loan Advisors help with?

Mortgage/LAP, corporate and business loans, working capital, term loans, structured finance, lease rental discounting, project finance and fund raising. Suitability depends on the borrower profile and lender policy.

Will my case be sent to every bank?

No. The preferred approach is to first understand and structure the case, then shortlist lenders that fit the borrower and transaction. Indiscriminate logging can create avoidable complications.

Can you guarantee sanction or the lowest interest rate?

No responsible advisor can guarantee a sanction or a particular rate. Final approval, pricing and conditions are decided by the lender. JR Loan Advisors focuses on strengthening the case, lender fit and execution.

Is my information confidential?

Borrower information is treated as confidential and used for evaluation and facilitation of the requested funding. Sensitive documents should be shared only through agreed channels after an initial discussion.
